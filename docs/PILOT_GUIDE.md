# PaisaFollow — Pilot user guide

_For the business owner or accountant. Takes 10 minutes to set up._

## Day 0 — Setup

1. **Sign up** with your email and confirm the link.
2. Go to **Settings → Business profile** and add your business name,
   timezone (India is already set), phone, and **UPI ID**. The UPI ID can be
   added to reminders so customers can pay you directly.
3. Go to **Receivables → Import**:
   - In Tally / Busy / Vyapar / Excel, export your **outstanding receivables**
     (party, bill no., bill date, due date, pending amount, mobile) and save
     the sheet as **CSV**.
   - Choose the file and read the preview. Red rows have a problem, with the
     reason shown. Yellow notes are warnings: for example, "same name as an
     existing customer". Nothing is merged without you seeing it.
   - Click **Import**. Re-importing the same file later is safe: existing
     invoice numbers are skipped.

## Every morning — 5 minutes

1. Open **Receivables → Today**. Customers are listed in the order to
   contact them, and each one shows **why**: days overdue, amount, missed
   promise, follow-up due.
2. For each customer:
   - **Send reminder**: pick the invoices, language (English / Hinglish /
     Hindi) and tone (polite / standard / firm), and check the preview. Then
     **Open WhatsApp** (or SMS / email / copy) and press send in your own app.
     Come back and tap **"Yes, mark as sent"**.
   - **Call**, then **Log outcome**: what they said, a **promise** (date +
     amount), or a **dispute** (with the reason). Set the **next follow-up
     date** if they asked you to call back.
3. When money arrives, open the invoice and **Record payment**: amount, date,
   UPI / NEFT / cash / cheque, and the reference. Part payments are fine.

## Good to know

- **Only real payments change balances.** "Customer says paid" is just a note
  until you record the payment.
- **Promises are tracked automatically.** If the promised amount hasn't
  arrived by the promised date, the customer comes back to the top of Today
  the next morning, marked as a missed promise.
- **Disputes are listed separately.** Solve the issue (short supply, rate
  difference, credit note) before sending reminders.
- **Mistakes**: you can't delete a payment. Use **Void** with a reason (for
  example, "cheque bounced") and record it again. Every change is kept in the
  audit trail.
- **Credit notes** reduce what the invoice owes without counting as cash
  received.
- **Your data is yours.** Reports → Export gives you CSVs of everything at any
  time. The owner can delete the account in Settings → Business profile.

## Messaging etiquette (please follow)

- Remind only customers you have an actual credit relationship with.
- Use the polite tone first. Firm is for long overdue invoices, and it stays
  professional: no threats, ever.
- If a customer asks you to stop messaging on WhatsApp, respect it and call
  or email instead.

## Known limitations in this pilot version

- Reminders are sent by you, from your own WhatsApp, SMS or email. Nothing is
  sent automatically yet.
- The interface is in English (reminder messages support Hindi and Hinglish).
- No PDF invoices. PaisaFollow tracks invoices made in your accounting
  software.
- Best for up to a few thousand invoices per business.
